KIMS IT SOLUTIONS

Modern School Management & Information System

KIMS IT SOLUTIONS is designed to support Uganda primary schools with school administration, student data, teacher management, marks entry, and printable report cards. It gives administrators and school staff a clear, centralized way to manage every academic term.

What it does

Capture school profiles, assign teachers, track student enrollment, and manage academic performance from one system.

How it works

Subject teachers upload marks to the class teacher, who compiles the mark list. The System Owner approves the school service payment before the school Admin generates report cards.

Why it helps

Reduce paperwork, improve accuracy, and make term reporting faster for teachers and administrators.

Getting started

  1. Read the system overview on this page.
  2. Click Login to open the login form.
  3. Sign in with the school account, then choose Subject Teacher, Class Teacher, or Admin.
